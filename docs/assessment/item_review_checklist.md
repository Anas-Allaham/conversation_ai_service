# Item review checklist

- Target level and communicative function are explicit.
- Wording is clear when spoken once.
- Topic is culturally neutral and does not require specialist facts.
- Task gives enough opportunity for speech at its level.
- It tests communication, not a hidden grammar rule.
- Normal item contains a fixed, genuinely interactive follow-up.
- Variants at the same level require comparable cognitive and language effort.
- Support and timing limits match the blueprint.
- Prompt is original and no third-party phrase was copied or lightly paraphrased.
- Metadata, version, provenance, author, reviewer, status, and notes are complete.
- Runtime status is `active` only after review.
- Pilot evidence and retirement decisions are preserved rather than overwritten.
